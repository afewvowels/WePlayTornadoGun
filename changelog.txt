Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-11-18
  Features:
    - New "Tornado size" map setting from 1 to 10, each step a third bigger (1 = 100%, 2 = 133%, 10 = 400%), defaulting to 2. It scales the tornado, how wide enemies orbit and how high they're lifted; the dust cloud grab radius keeps its own setting.
    - Physical projectile damage research now boosts the tornado's swirl and fall damage, including the infinite levels.
  Balancing:
    - Swirl damage is now physical damage, so enemies' physical resistances apply, the same as against bullets. Fall damage stays impact damage.
  Bugfixes:
    - Big biters now ride all the way up the funnel. Lifted enemies no longer collide with anything, so trees, rocks and other enemies can't hold them near the ground.
  Optimizations:
    - Map settings are cached instead of read several times per swirling enemy every tick.
  Changes:
    - New mod description.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-11
  Major Features:
    - First public release: a tornado gun that fires tornadoes which grow out of a swirling dust ball, sweep up enemies, spin them through the funnel and drop them hard.
    - Unlocked by the new Tornado gun technology, after Military 3.
  Balancing:
    - Default max distance lowered to 30 tiles (was 50), and gun range to match.
    - Swirl damage back on by default: 25 per second on average while enemies are lifted.
    - Tornado gun recipe: 3 engine units, 10 steel plate, 10 copper plate and a barrel.
    - Tornado charge recipe: 5 copper plate, 2 stone, a barrel and 100 steam; crafted in an assembling machine 2 or 3.
